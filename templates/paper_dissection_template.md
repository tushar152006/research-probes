# Paper Dissection Rubric (Your Authentic Voice)

> **Rule**: Do not write what the paper does; write what the paper *means*, why it works (or doesn't), and where it breaks.

---

## 1. The Core Idea (Explain to a smart peer in 2 minutes)
- What is the simple insight behind the math?
- What problem were they actually trying to solve?

## 2. The Clever Move
- What specific trick, loss function, or architecture change made this stand out?
- Why didn't previous approaches think of this?

## 3. The Skeptic's Corner (Crucial)
- What unstated assumptions are they taking for granted?
- Did they pick weak baselines or tune them poorly?
- Where does this break down in the real world?
- If I change input distribution X or constraint Y, does it fail?

## 4. The Micro-Experiment (Proof of Work)
- What 30-80 line Python script or Colab did I run?
- What did the empirical numbers/plot show? Did it corroborate their claim or reveal a hitch?

## 5. The Open Question (What's Next?)
- What is the immediate next step someone should build or fix?
- What would I ask the primary author if we were having coffee?
